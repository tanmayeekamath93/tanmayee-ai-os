# AI quality rubric

Use this to evaluate whether an AI output is strong enough to use without significant rework.

## Criteria

### 1. Relevance
- Does the answer address the actual request?
- Is it aligned to the likely user goal?

### 2. Clarity
- Is it easy to understand?
- Is the structure scannable and practical?

### 3. Actionability
- Does it provide a clear recommendation or next step?
- Can the user make a decision from it?

### 4. Evidence and judgment
- Does it separate fact from assumption?
- Does it avoid unsupported certainty?

### 5. Completeness
- Does it cover the major dimensions of the problem?
- Are important trade-offs or risks surfaced?

## Scoring

Rate each criterion from 1 to 5:

- 1 = poor
- 2 = weak
- 3 = acceptable
- 4 = strong
- 5 = excellent

## Pass bar

A result is usually acceptable when:

- average score is 4 or above
- no critical criterion falls below 3
- clear recommendation or action is present

## Red flags

- generic or inflated answer
- no clear recommendation
- unsupported certainty
- missing assumptions or trade-offs
- answer is longer than needed without more value
