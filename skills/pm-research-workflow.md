# PM + research workflow

This is the first reusable workflow skill for product and research work.

## When to use

Use this workflow when you need to:

- turn vague inputs into a clear product or research brief
- synthesize notes, interviews, or scattered ideas
- shape a plan, recommendation, or decision memo
- create a structured output without repeating the same instructions in chat

## Workflow

### 1. Frame the task

Define:

- the problem or decision to be made
- the user or stakeholder context
- the output needed
- the core constraints

### 2. Gather and sort input

Identify:

- facts
- assumptions
- open questions
- signals or evidence

### 3. Synthesize

Convert the above into:

- key themes
- important patterns
- likely opportunities or risks
- implications for action

### 4. Recommend

Provide a prioritized recommendation with:

- what to do
- why it matters
- what trade-offs exist
- what success should look like

### 5. Convert to execution

Turn the recommendation into a usable output such as:

- a concise brief
- an issue or opportunity statement
- a roadmap or sequencing recommendation
- a next-step plan

## Output template

Use this structure:

### Objective
What decision or problem are we solving?

### Context
What do we know, and what assumptions are we making?

### Findings
What patterns or insights stand out?

### Recommendation
What should be done and why?

### Risks and trade-offs
What could go wrong, and what are the trade-offs?

### Next steps
What should happen next, and in what order?

## Quality bar

A strong output should be:

- specific enough to act on
- grounded in evidence or reasonable inference
- explicit about assumptions
- easy to scan and share

## Prompt pattern

Use this as a reusable prompt frame:

"Use the personal AI operating instructions and the PM + research workflow. Frame the problem, identify what is known vs unknown, synthesize the key patterns, recommend the best next action, and present the outcome in a concise, decision-oriented format."
