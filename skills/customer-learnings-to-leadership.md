# Customer learnings to leadership

Use this skill when you need to turn release metrics and customer feedback into a concise, leadership-ready summary.

## Purpose

Create a brief that helps leadership understand:

- what shipped
- how customers are using it
- what is working well
- where friction remains
- what product or go-to-market adjustments are recommended

## Output expectations

Produce an HTML review document that is easy to scan in a leadership setting.

## Required structure

1. Executive summary
2. Key takeaways
3. Customer learning summary
4. Feature usage snapshot
5. Customer quotes or signals
6. Implications for leadership
7. Recommended next actions

## Working method

### Step 1: Scope the review
Define:

- feature name
- release window
- audience
- business question or decision being informed

### Step 2: Summarize customer behavior
Capture:

- adoption patterns
- retention or engagement trend
- pain points and blockers
- notable positive signals

### Step 3: Turn insights into leadership framing
Explain what the usage data means in business terms:

- what is working
- what is lagging
- what should be prioritized next

### Step 4: Write the HTML summary
Use a polished leadership style with:

- a crisp executive summary
- clear metric callouts
- short bullets and insights
- simple tables for usage metrics
- decision-oriented recommendations

## Quality bar

The output should be:

- easy to consume in 5-10 minutes
- narrative but concise
- grounded in data and customer language
- useful for leadership review and follow-up discussion

## Prompt pattern

"Create a leadership-ready HTML summary of customer learnings and feature usage for this release. Include an executive summary, key takeaways, usage snapshots, customer insights, implications for leadership, and recommended next actions. Keep the tone concise, clear, and decision-oriented, and format it so it is easy to review in a leadership meeting."
