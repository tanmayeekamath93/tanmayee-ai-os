# Decision memo template

## Decision
What decision are we making, and why now?

## Context
- Problem or opportunity
- Goal or desired outcome
- Relevant constraints
- Timeline and stakeholders

## Options considered
### Option 1
- Summary
- Pros
- Cons
- When this option fits

### Option 2
- Summary
- Pros
- Cons
- When this option fits

## Recommendation
State the recommended path and why it is the best fit.

## Trade-offs
- What we gain
- What we give up
- What we need to watch closely

## Risks and assumptions
- Key risks
- Key assumptions
- Unknowns that require validation

## Next steps
1. Immediate action
2. Follow-on step
3. Decision checkpoint or review
